# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Rustix project is committed to providing an open, welcoming and respectful environment for everyone who participates in its development and community.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels, backgrounds and skill sets. Whether you're an experienced systems programmer or making your first contribution, your participation is valued.

## Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, considerate and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and help others learn.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing opinions, experiences and approaches.
- Communicate professionally and in good faith.
- Give credit where credit is due.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Insulting or demeaning language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or project development.
- Any other behaviour considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

Disagreements about technical decisions are perfectly acceptable. Personal hostility is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Rustix project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Project documentation and communication channels.
- Any other space where someone is representing the Rustix project.

It also applies when someone is officially representing Rustix in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Reports should include relevant context and, where possible, links or evidence of the behaviour.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, consequences may include:

1. A warning and request to stop the behaviour.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate contributions or content.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Enforcement decisions will be made at the maintainer's discretion, with the aim of protecting the project's community.
